## Evaluation protocol

1. **Freeze reference inputs and gates.** Use the owner’s private, consented labelled data where available. Start from the proposed 30-meeting design with at least 10 per primary stratum (English, Chinese, code-switching/Singlish), including at least 10 meetings of 60 minutes or more. Add a separately identified Malay and Malay/English slice subject to consent and data availability. Match by meeting and speaker group across development, validation, and sealed test splits. Use two independent annotators and a documented adjudication process. Keep reference turns, overlaps, timing, exact or normalized transcript, uncertainty, and critical facts with the locked rubric. Record an unavailable stratum explicitly. See [quality evaluation](QUALITY-EVALUATION.md#evaluation-surface-and-reference-set).
2. **Verify deterministic and browser contracts first.** Mock authenticated egress and faults for cancellation before and after submission, truncated streams, unsupported format, interrupted local save, and reload/restore. Check identical source and artifact preservation, exact citation resolution, zero automatic duplicate submissions after unknown outcomes, and consent invalidation for recipient or payload changes. Perform real provider qualification only with separate authorization, explicit content consent, and the target account. On the actual production origin check preflight and the authenticated request, returned metadata, observed upstream when disclosed, limits, and actual usage. Measure a valid smaller fixture before testing bounded long input. Record API and app caps separately, with live failures classified without inducing paid faults. Verify Chromium/WebKit and physical iPhone and Android, including installed/offline playback for saved audio.
3. **Score transcription and diarization independently.** Run the frozen Scribe baseline and each eligible candidate on paired inputs with the same chunk boundaries and no silent fallback. Report source integrity and coverage, WER/CER, critical term and number errors, timing error/coverage, diarization error components (miss, false alarm, confusion), speaker counts, and attribution/overlap uncertainty. Report diarization within chunks separately from any acoustic cross-chunk mapping, with meeting-local anonymous labels and no real-person identity claims. Keep unclear regions explicit. Run downstream memo scoring once with the reference transcript and once with each candidate’s actual output to distinguish recognition from reasoning errors.
4. **Score narration independently of script drafting.** Freeze the approved, cited script and compare stock voices on identical text. Blind listeners to route identity. Score omissions/additions, accuracy of owners, dates, numbers, uncertainty, conditions and quotations, pronunciation and intelligibility for each stratum, speaker-role separation, and audio/chapter alignment. Have reviewers identify possible participant resemblance. Separately test LLM script citations, critical-field support and recall against the frozen source references under the project's proposed gates. Retain the transcript or hearing annotations for the rendered output and record corrections and usefulness scores. Apply the quality contract's human review and critical-failure gates before promotion.
5. **Report and decide.** Ratify per-language speech thresholds and comparisons against the frozen baseline before candidate scoring. Repeat a representative subset at least three times to capture nondeterminism. Give meeting-level macro results, supplemental counts and denominators, unknown and excluded cases, per-language/length slices, meeting-cluster confidence intervals, P50/P95 latency and tested resource and failure envelopes. Record build SHA, dataset/rubric hashes, requested route and actual model/upstream/version when available, prompt/adapter versions, request outcomes, recovery evidence and human adjudication. Track reported usage and charges separately from estimates and quality. Promote only after measured quality, verified browser/device gates and paid-recovery checks pass. Record `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_RUN` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` per gate. No candidate has a measured ranking in this exploration. [Evidence packet](QUALITY-EVALUATION.md#evidence-packet-schema)
